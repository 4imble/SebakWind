# Backstory: The Crow in the Wind

### **Lineage & Early Life**

Born in the quiet, rain-slicked border towns of the Land of Fire, Sebak was a secret kept even from the elders of Konohagakure. His mother was a civilian herbalist from a forgotten minor clan in the river valleys who tended to a wounded, exhausted ANBU captain before the night of the Uchiha massacre. She never spoke the name **Uchiha Itachi** aloud, leaving only a single, worn crow feather tucked inside a wooden amulet and a warning: *never let anyone see your eyes if they turn red.*  
They never did. While Sebak inherited his father's sharp, melancholic features, dark hair, and preternatural tactical mind, the Sharingan remained dormant. Instead, whenever Sebak was pushed to the brink of death or extreme mental strain, something else stirred in his blood: a strange, heavy resonance from his mother's forgotten lineage that made the physical matter around him hum and vibrate, though it never fully manifested.  
Trained in the shadows by wandering mercenaries and relying on pure shinobi fundamentals (stealth, blade work, and razor-sharp chakra control), Sebak lived as a ghost on the fringes of the shinobi world.

### **The Transition (Isekai)**

At age seventeen, while investigating an ancient, crumbling shrine in the mountains to escape hunter-nin tracking remnants of bloodline limits, Sebak triggered a dormant spatial seal. Expecting an explosive tag, he poured his chakra into a defensive Shunshin (Body Flicker). Instead of ash and fire, the seal tore open a rift in the void. His chakra violently collided with the spatial tear, pulling him through a silent, starless expanse.  
He awoke beneath a canopy of towering, unfamiliar pines, the air smelling of woodsmoke, autumn, and iron. There was no natural energy of the Elemental Nations here. The ambient chakra of the world was gone, leaving only the reserves coiled tightly within his own body.

### **Arrival in Temerant**

Sebak found himself in the Commonwealth, near the borders of the Eld, eventually making his way toward the twin cities of Imre and the University. He quickly learned the rules of this new world:

> * **Currency & Survival:** Ryo was worthless; talents, jots, and drabs ruled. His shinobi skills made him an exceptional scout, bodyguard, and thief when coin ran dry.  
> * **The Nature of Magic:** There were no hand seals here. Instead, he witnessed an Arcanist fend off highwaymen by muttering a binding and holding a copper penny over a candle, setting a bandit's belt ablaze.

With an innate genius inherited from his father, Sebak immediately grasped the underlying logic of **Sympathy**. To a mind already trained to split focus between chakra moulding, spatial awareness, and tactical prediction, the mental discipline of **Alar** (riding-crop belief) came with terrifying ease. Furthermore, he made a groundbreaking personal discovery: his internal **Chakra** acts as a potent, self-generating thermal and kinetic source, though drawing link energy directly from his own body carries the deadly risk of **Binder's Chills**.
